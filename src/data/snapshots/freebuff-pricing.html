While Freebuff is in beta

  • A model's Freebucks cost can vary. It tracks what that model costs to run, so it moves with how the model is used and with what its provider charges. A change never affects a session already running.
  • Limits can change. Daily and monthly figures are tuned as we learn what the plans cost to run, and can be tightened to stop abuse.
  • You will always get more usage than you pay for. Whatever the limits are on any given day, a plan is sized to return more in model spend than it costs — that is the one thing we will not change.
  • Fair-use limits still apply. Usage limits can prevent new sessions or stop a running session, even with allowance remaining.

Cancel any time. Cancelling a yearly plan stops its next renewal; your access and perks continue through the paid year, with no automatic refund. Cancelling a monthly plan ends your plan and its perks immediately, with no proration or refund. A year paid once never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.
